iPhone

Download on the App Store

Free. Search the App Store for Hound: Fitness with Friends if the button doesn't open it.

Android

Get it on Google Play

Free. Search Google Play for Hound: Fitness with Friends if the button doesn't open it. Updates come through Google Play like any other app.

Installed Hound from our APK before?

Google Play can't update that copy. Uninstall Hound, install it from Google Play, then sign in the same way as before (Google, Apple or email): your challenges, friends and hound are all saved to your account.

Can't use Google Play? Download the APK instead

Google Play is the safest way to get Hound: it checks the app and keeps it up to date. Use the APK only if your phone has no Google Play.

Download the APK (Hound 1.2.0)

  • It comes from Google Drive, which can't virus-scan app files, so it asks before downloading. If the file opens in the Drive app, use ⋮ → Download.
  • Android asks before installing an app from outside Google Play. Allow it for this one install, and turn that setting back off afterwards (Settings → Apps → Special access → Install unknown apps).
  • If your phone won't allow it (Samsung's Auto Blocker, or a work phone), please use Google Play instead. We'd rather you didn't turn your phone's protections off.
