What to expect during install

  • Allow installs from your browser. Android blocks installs from outside the Play Store by default. When it warns you, tap Settings on that prompt, turn on Allow permission for your browser (Samsung Internet, Chrome, etc.), then go back and open the APK again.
  • Turn off Auto Blocker. Even after allowing your browser above, Samsung's Auto Blocker shows an "Unknown app blocked" dialog and stops the install anyway. Go to Settings → Security and privacy → Auto Blocker and turn it off, then install right away — Auto Blocker automatically turns itself back on after about 30 minutes.
  • Turning Auto Blocker back on afterward is fine — it only affects installing new apps from outside the Play Store, not apps you already have installed.
