The Hound guide

Everything you can do in Hound

Hound turns the steps, walks and workouts your phone already counts into games with your friends and a hound that lives the day with you. Here's all of it, and where to find each thing in the app.

    iPhone: get it free on the App Store. Android: get it free on Google Play.

    You → Health data
  3. 3

Challenges

25 kinds, all played on the steps, miles and workouts you'd do anyway. Some unlock once you've earned enough Bones; the lock shows how many.

Races and targets

Step Race

Most steps during the challenge. Everyone against everyone.

Any group · Bones pot optional

Group Distance Target

Every mile the group covers adds up toward one shared target.

A team effort

Daily Streak

Hit a daily goal every day. One miss and you're out.

Last one standing

Chase

One runner gets a head start. The other has to catch them on logged miles before time runs out.

Hunter and hunted

Game of Tag

One hound is IT each day. Everyone else gets a head start; whoever IT out-walks is tagged, and the slowest of them is IT tomorrow. +3 a tag, +1 a day you get away. Watch each day back on the track.

Any group

Journey

Everyone walks the same famous course from the same start. Walks, runs and hikes move you along the map, with stops and postcards on the way. First to the end wins.

Golden Gate Bridge 1.7 mi · Windy City, Five Boroughs and Thames 26.2 · Death Valley 135 · Appalachian Trail 2,190 · Route 66 2,448

Games

Tic-Tac-Go

Two players, one board. Hit a square's goal to claim it, then it's their move. Three in a row wins; once neither of you can get three in a row, it's a draw.

2 players · Bones pot optional

Bingo

A card of steps, workouts and goals. First to finish a row, column or diagonal wins, and play goes on for the full card.

Any group

Hound'opoly

A property board game on your steps. Each day's steps pick your dice, the roll happens overnight, and you buy the parks and trails you land on. Richest at the end wins.

Any group

Rally

Badminton on your steps. Walk the shuttle's step target before its deadline to send it back a little bigger. Miss and they score. Games to 21, best of three.

One on one

Soccer

A match on everyone's steps, a day at a time. Forwards shoot, defenders block, the keeper saves. Most goals after the last day wins.

Teams of 1 to 9 · friends or bots

Hound Chess

Real chess with hounds, against a friend or the Hound bot. Your steps free your pieces: knights and bishops at 2,500, rooks at 5,000, the queen at 7,500 or after a 20-minute workout, on today's steps or yesterday's, whichever is more. In check, everything's free. 24 hours a move. Plus a mate-in-one puzzle every day once you reach 5,000 steps.

1 on 1 · a friend or the bot · 3 to 14 days

Paw Slots

A slot machine played on your steps: every 500 steps is a spin (up to 20 a day, plus 2 for a 20-minute workout), and spare Paw Coins buy a few more. Play 1, 3 or 5 lines at up to ×5 a line; three Golden Hounds pays 250, and the Collar is wild and doubles a win. The odds are in Help. When it ends, the prize pool is paid by place, solo or by pack. Money and Bones never go in.

A group, solo or in 2 to 4 packs · 3 to 21 days

Hound Roulette

Pick a stake (Safe, Bold or All in) and spin: the wheel gives you today's task, sized to you. Do it and the points ride; bank them before tomorrow's spin or let them ride for a bigger multiplier. Miss and the ride's gone. Raid a friend who's riding high, or call them out to a Duel for your points. Most banked wins the Lucky Bandana, and every banked point is a Bone.

Solo, 1 on 1 or a group · 3 to 21 days

Cricket

Limited overs, one over a day. Each ball is the batter's steps against the bowler's: out-walk them for boundaries, fall behind and wickets fall. Turn on Bat and bowl yourself to play your balls: tap the ball to bat, or pick a delivery and your spot to bowl.

Teams of 1 to 9 · friends or bots

Baseball

One inning a day. Each at-bat is your steps against the pitcher's, then the fielder's, then the catcher's at home plate. Aim left, center or right; the catcher calls a shift; big step days clear the fence. With Take my own at-bats (on for new games) you play it yourself: one at-bat per 3,000 steps, up to six a day, for batting and pitching. At the plate, read the grip in the pitcher's glove (fastball, curveball or changeup), then tap where the ball crosses the plate as it gets there: where you tap is the swing, when you tap is the timing. On the mound, pick a pitch, tap your spot and let it go as the ring closes. Once a day, set your jump to steal second. Balls, strikes and fouls count, early pulls it and late goes the other way, and a strength workout adds power. Each at-bat counts when its batter next comes up; everything else plays overnight.

Teams of 1 to 9 · friends or bots · 3 to 9 innings

Hoops

Basketball, a quarter a day. Your steps take the shots, one every 2,000 or so, up to five a day. Pick a drive, a mid-range or a three, out-walk whoever's guarding you to make them fall, and vote on your team's defense: pack the paint or guard the arc. Big days are dunks; three in a row and you're on fire. With Take my own shots (on for new games) you take them yourself as your steps earn them: pick the shot, tap Shoot, and release in the green. Your defender's steps narrow the green. Shots you don't take still play for you overnight.

Teams of 1 to 9 · friends or bots · 4 or 8 days

H-O-R-S-E

One on one: set a shot, and the other has to match it or take a letter. Spell H-O-R-S-E (or P-I-G) and you're out. With Take my own shots (on for new games) you call your shot: tap anywhere on the court or up on the bench, chain your calls (off the floor, off the scoreboard, off the glass, behind the back, nothing but net), then release in the green. Each call narrows it, and so does shooting over the day's chair in the lane. Turns pass back and forth, 24 hours each, one shot per call; miss your own call and the turn passes. Tries come from your steps, one per 2,000, and a big day unlocks spots for the rest of the game: the stands at 4,000, the rafters at 7,000, the parking lot (through the gym window) at 10,000, and a rooftop across the street at 15,000. On your last letter you get two tries. Four- and five-star makes go to the challenge's Talk, and with stakes on, the loser bakes the winner's hound a treat. Hands-off games still play on step targets: from a 6,000-step layup to a 15,000-step half-court heave.

2 players · 24-hour turns · stakes optional

Long Shot

Steel plates at 300 to 3,000 yards. Every 1,000 steps is a round (up to 15 a day), plus one for every 20 minutes of workout, up to 5. Read the three wind flags, hold off in the scope, and fire in the natural pause of your breath; a workout steadies your hold. Hound flies every round for real, with the day's air and wind the same for everyone. Center hits double, the gold plate pays Bones, your data card learns from your hits, and Moon days have a sixth of the gravity.

A group · rifles unlock with steps · prize pool by points

Bullseye

Archery for families. Every 1,500 steps is a round of three arrows, up to four rounds a day (twelve arrows), and up to six unshot arrows carry over to tomorrow. Drag to aim (read the wind flags first), hold to draw, and let go while the dot is steadiest. Shoot one arrow at a time, or a volley of two or three off one draw: quicker, but only the first follows your aiming dot. Targets from 18 to 90 m score 1 to 10 times the target's ×, the flag at 150 m is clout, and splitting your own arrow is a Robin Hood. A gold balloon each day, Balloon days on Wednesdays, Moon days on Saturdays.

A group · youth bow, longbow, recurve; the compound after 50,000 steps

Hound Bout

Boxing, one on one, a round a day. Every 1,500 steps is an action, up to 10 a day: throw an exchange (tap each opening as its ring closes: jab, hook or uppercut) or face one of their combinations and slip it. Set your guard each day, counter for stars, and land a three-punch power combination for a knockdown. Rounds are scored the next morning; three knockdowns in a round is a KO.

1 on 1 · 3, 5 or 7 rounds · Auto when you're short on time

Golf

Nine or eighteen holes, and you take every shot. Every 10 steps bank a yard; workouts bank power (strength counts double, golf triple). Pull down on the hole to aim, the caddie picks the club, and tap Hit as the marker crosses the middle. Each shot spends what it used. Four courses, every hole different, with bunkers, water and wind. Stroke play, Skins or Match play.

Just you or any group · 4 days (9 holes) or a week (18)

Ways to play

Global Hound Challenges

Open to everyone on Hound: join from Today while it's open, no invite needed. Some have Bones prizes for the top 3, and some are sponsored.

Today → Global Hound Challenges

The Hound Open

Golf with everyone on Hound: one course over a week, stroke play, every shot your own. Join in the first three days; your steps and workouts count from the day you join. The leaderboard flips between the top of the field, your friends and your club, and the top three win Bones.

Today → Global Hound Challenges, while one's on

The Home Run Derby

A week at the plate with everyone on Hound. One swing per 1,000 steps, up to 20 a day; every pitch is a meatball near the middle: tap the ball where it crosses, right on time. Your steps today and a strength workout add distance, up to 500 feet; 475 feet or more is a Moonshot and counts twice. One of your first ten pitches each day is a golden ball, and a second shows up after 15,000 steps: a home run off one counts double. Hit the sponsor's sign on the center-field wall for bonus Bones. The pitcher speeds up as the week goes on. Most home runs wins (ties go to the longest), with prizes for the top three, 4th to 10th, the longest home run, and everyone with 10 or more.

Today → Global Hound Challenges, while one's on → Step up to the plate

Title Bout

Hound Bout for everyone, for a week. Days 1 to 5 are the ladder: a new opponent each day, matched by where you are, one round each. Then the top two fight for the belt over two rounds. Anyone else can pick the champion after reading the tale of the tape; a right pick earns Bones, double for the underdog. The champion's hound wears the belt.

Today → Global Hound Challenges, while one's on

The Shootout

A week of penalty kicks with everyone. One kick per 1,000 steps, up to 20 a day: tap where to aim, then Kick! as the power marker crosses the green. Top corners count 2, a golden ball counts double, and the keeper dives where you go most, so mix it up. A strength workout puts pace on your kicks.

Today → Global Hound Challenges, while one's on

Pack Wars

A Global Hound Challenge for 7 to 30 days. You're put in one of four packs (Wolves, Golden Retrievers, Border Collies or Beagles), each starting on a corner of the neighborhood map. Each day, vote where your pack marches, or Hold to defend. Only members who vote add their steps that day, and overnight your pack takes the territory if it's the strongest there and beats the owner's defence. Most ground at the end wins, and the winning pack gets Bones.

Today → Global Hound Challenges → Pack Wars

Daily Doors

A door for every day. Open today's for 1 point, then do the task behind it by midnight for 2 more: your steps target (a little over last week's average), a workout, your water, all your goals, a treat for your hound, a 10-minute walk, or a cheer for someone. Hound checks it for you. Three done in a row and each one after is worth 1 more, and one missed day a week doesn't break your run. Bones behind some doors, a frame you can't buy behind the last.

Today → Global Hound Challenges → Daily Doors

Clubs

Run clubs, cycling groups and walkers. Find a public club by name, city or sport, or join a private one with its code, link or QR. A club's page shows the challenges members have posted (with a Join button), how the club did this week, and who's in it. Share a challenge with your clubs and every member gets the invite. Start your own once you reach the level it asks for (up to four); clubs show usernames, so you'll need one to join. Tap the bell on a club's page to mute it. A Fasting club leads with who's fasting now and the week's board, and Hound clubs, like Intermittent Fasting, are open to everyone with no member limit.

Clubs tab · New challenge → Share with your clubs

Pack Activities

A live session for a walk, run or hike together. Start one, pick when and how long, and invite friends or open it to your club. While it's on, the board shows everyone's steps, scent-trail puzzles open as the pack's steps add up, and anyone can start a quick Dash or Pack push. Turn on the live map to see each other and Bones to walk to; your spot is shared with that pack only, and deleted when it ends.

Clubs tab or a club's page → Start a Pack Activity · Today → Needs you

Community challenges

Publish a challenge that anyone with its code, link or QR code can join. Members from Tracker (level 5) with a username can publish.

Challenges → Join → Have a code?

Bones pots

Put 10, 25, 50 or 100 Bones each on a Step Race or Tic-Tac-Go. Winner takes all; a tie splits it. Only Bones earned by playing can go in.

New challenge → Step Race or Tic-Tac-Go → Pot

Boards, talk and rematches

  • Each challenge has its Board (standings, with ▲▼ for who moved since yesterday), a Talk wall for trash talk, and Details.
  • Pin one to the top of Today.
  • When it ends: Rematch with the same friends and rules, or Share result with an invite link.
Tap a challenge → Board · Talk · Details

Goals and water

Goals

Steps, distance, workouts or workout minutes, every day or every week. Hound suggests a target a little above your last four weeks.

You → Goals

Log a workout

No watch? Log a workout yourself: up to two a day, up to 90 minutes each, today or yesterday. It counts in full for your goals and Daily Doors, and at half value in the sports games and Golf. It never adds steps or miles, and it's marked "Logged by hand" (you can undo it for 15 minutes).

Today → Goals → Log a workout

The Hound reminds you

Choose how hard Hound hounds you: Gentle (one evening reminder), Persistent (up to 3 a day), Relentless (up to 5) or Off. Reminders stop once your goals are done.

You → Goals

Goal streak and Streak Shields

Every goal day pays Bones, and every 7th day in a row pays more. A Streak Shield covers one missed day. Finish every weekly goal for a weekly bonus.

Today → Goals

Water

A daily goal in cups or millilitres, a glass at a time, plus water other apps put in Health. Optional sip reminders, and your hound's bowl fills through the day.

Today → Goals → the blue +

Hound on Apple Watch

Your goal rings and streak on your wrist, and a glass of water with one tap. Open Hound on the watch, then enter the six-letter code it shows on your iPhone. Add Hound to a watch face as a complication.

You → Settings → Apple Watch

  • Today's step rank, the rest of the week.
  • Personal records: best step day, longest 10k streak, longest workout, farthest day.
  • Home screen widgets (from 0.11.0): your hound and today's goals, and steps on the iPhone Lock Screen.
Today · You → Your data

Fasting

Start a fast

Tap Start when you finish eating. Pick a plan, from 12:12 to one meal a day (16:8 is the most popular), and if you stopped eating a while ago, set the time, up to 12 hours back. Started by mistake? Cancel in the first 15 minutes. It's for people 16 and older, with a one-time check: talk to your doctor first if you're pregnant or breastfeeding, take insulin or diabetes medicine, or have had an eating disorder.

Today → Goals → Try intermittent fasting · after your first fast, the Fasting tile on Today

The dial and the stages

The dial fills to your goal through the stages, each in its own colour with a badge where it begins: settling (4 hours), fat burning (12) and ketosis (16). Next up says what's coming and when, up to your goal. Your phone tells you as each stage begins, when you reach your goal, and checks in if you're still going at 36 hours. The stages sheet says how sure the science is for each.

Fasting → the dial · Stages

What to drink

  • Yes: water (still or sparkling), black coffee, plain tea, unsweetened electrolytes.
  • Your call: diet soda, a splash of milk, bone broth.
  • Breaks a fast: milk or cream, sugar or honey, juice, butter coffee, protein shakes, alcohol.
Fasting → What to drink

End it, and Bones

Tap End fast when you eat, or set when you ate. Ending early still counts as a fast, just not on plan. Bones once a day for the first fast that ends that day: 5 at 12 hours, 10 at 14, 15 at 16, 20 at 18 and 25 at 20, and nothing more after that. Streak bonuses at 3, 7, 14, 30 and 100 days on plan in a row. Pick up to three rest days a week, and one missed day a week doesn't break the streak.

Fasting → End fast

With friends

  • Fasting now: friends who are fasting, with Cheer (they get a push).
  • Pack fasts: Start together invites up to 10 friends; when everyone who joined ends on plan, you all get a bonus. Quick notes keep the pack going.
  • The board: you and your friends by days on plan this week, then streak. Longest fasts of 24 hours or more show "24 h+" until tapped. Tap a friend to compare the last 30 days.
  • Clubs: the Intermittent Fasting club with everyone on Hound, or a Fasting club of your own.
  • Rather keep it to yourself? Turn off "Friends and clubmates see when I'm fasting".
Fasting · Clubs → Hound clubs

Your hound

A hound who lives the day with you, in its own yard.

Mood and care

Its mood follows your goal days over the last week. Give a treat once a day. Gentle care is the default; Classic pet adds meal hearts to keep filled.

Today → your hound · or You → Kennel

Its look and wardrobe

  • Coat, ears, tail, markings and breeds like Poodle and French Bulldog.
  • Collars, bandanas, hats and glasses; sweaters, a hoodie, a raincoat, a tutu, a superhero cape, a tuxedo; booties and sneakers.
  • Holiday drops for a little while each season (Thanksgiving, Winter from 15 November). Anything you get, you keep.
  • Tap anything to try it on in place: red ✕ puts it back, green ✓ keeps it.
Kennel → Wardrobe · Look

The Yard

It grows with your rank. Your hound strolls, naps in bed or the hammock, and drinks from a bowl that fills with your water. Swipe the grass to throw its toy. Trophies you win in challenges stand in it.

Kennel → Yard

Kennel → swipe the sky across, or Yard → Porch

The room

Inside the house, open to everyone: a Fireplace (lit in the evening and all winter), Sofa, Bookshelf, Photo Wall with your hound's portrait, Fireside Cushion, Cozy Rug, Floor Lamp and Potted Fern, from 150 to 900 Bones.

Kennel → swipe on past the Porch, or Yard → Room

Fitness, traits, age and a birthday

  • Fitness 1 to 10 from active days (5,000 steps). It only goes up, and a fitter hound is livelier.
  • Nine traits from your habits, kept for good: Early bird, Night owl, Lunch stroller, Trail dog, Well watered, Weekend warrior, Steady paws, Collector, Green paws.
  • A new hound starts as a puppy and grows up with your steps.
  • Pick its birthday. On the day, the yard has a party and a friend's hound comes round.

Walks, care and friends

Your walks become your hound's walks, with nothing to start or stop.

Walk recaps

Ten minutes or more of steady steps is a walk: "Morning walk · 8:06 AM · 1.5 mi · 32 min".

Kennel → Walks

Walk finds and the collection book

Every finished walk brings something home, up to 5 a day. 22 kinds: a good stick, a feather, an old tennis ball… and seasonal ones like a golden acorn or a perfect snowflake. Longer walks find rarer things.

Kennel → Walks → Open the collection book

Your hound's diary

A line a day in its own voice, from your walks, finds, steps and water: "A long evening walk: 3.7 mi. My paws are tired." Only you see it, unless you share the week.

Kennel → Walks → Diary

The garden

Three plots in the Yard. Plant a seed and your steps water it: a daisy blooms at 20,000 steps, a giant sunflower at 70,000. A day off pauses it; nothing wilts. One free seed a week, and seed packs for Bones.

Kennel → Yard → Garden

Bath day

A long walk (30 minutes or 3,000 steps) brings your hound home muddy. A busy day (that walk, 8,000 steps or your goals done) earns a bath with bubbles, once a day, and they stay fresh until the next long walk.

Kennel → Care

Homemade treats

Bake Pumpkin biscuits, a Peanut butter pupcake, Frosty paws or a Birthday cake from your walk finds plus a few Bones. Feed one as today's treat, or send it to a friend's hound with a note.

Kennel → Care → Kitchen

Playdates

On a day you and a friend have both finished your goals, their hound comes over to play in your yard. Friends you're in a challenge with come first.

Kennel → Yard

Walks together and the dog park

When your walk overlaps a friend's by 10 minutes or more, it says who with. The dog park shows friends' hounds that have been out walking in the last day, with yours once you've walked. Tap one to visit.

Kennel → Walks → Dog park

Rewards

Hound Score, XP and ranks

Hound Score grows with your results and never goes down. XP from playing, opening Hound and your goals fills your level. Ranks: Pup, Tracker (level 5), Hunter (10), Alpha (15), Legend (20).

You

Moon forms

Each new rank is a transformation under the moon, with its own cut scene: First Moon at Tracker (level 5), Forest Moon at Hunter (10), Full Moon at Alpha (15) and Blue Moon at Legend (20). Your hound keeps each change: a crescent on its brow, a spiky mane, glowing eyes and a moonlight aura. Watch any form you've reached again; the ones ahead show the level they open at. "Show my hound's moon look" turns the look off.

Kennel → your hound → Moon forms
