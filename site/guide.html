The Hound guide

Everything you can do in Hound

Hound turns the steps, walks and workouts your phone already counts into games with your friends and a hound that lives the day with you. Here's all of it, and where to find each thing in the app.

Get started

  1. 1

    Get the app

    iPhone: get it free on the App Store. Android: download the beta from houndchallenge.net/get.

  2. 2

    Connect your health data

    Hound reads steps, distance and workouts from Apple Health or Health Connect, so anything your phone or watch counts, counts.

    You → Health data
  3. 3

    Set your goals and meet your hound

    Everyone starts with 10,000 steps a day, 3 miles a day and 3 workouts a week. Make them yours, then name your hound.

    Today → Set your goals
  4. 4

    Start or join a challenge

    Pick a game, set the dates and invite friends, or join a Global Hound Challenge from Today.

    Challenges → New challenge
  5. 5

    Turn on notifications

    So you hear about invites, your Tic-Tac-Go turn, nudges and the end of a challenge.

    You → Settings → Notifications

Challenges

17 kinds, all played on the steps, miles and workouts you'd do anyway. Some unlock once you've earned enough Bones; the lock shows how many.

Races and targets

Step Race

Most steps during the challenge. Everyone against everyone.

Any group · Bones pot optional

Group Distance Target

Every mile the group covers adds up toward one shared target.

A team effort

Daily Streak

Hit a daily goal every day. One miss and you're out.

Last one standing

Chase

One runner gets a head start. The other has to catch them on logged miles before time runs out.

Hunter and hunted

Game of Tag

One hound is IT each day. Everyone else gets a head start; whoever IT out-walks is tagged, and the slowest of them is IT tomorrow. +3 a tag, +1 a day you get away. Watch each day back on the track.

Any group

Journey

Everyone walks the same famous course from the same start. Walks, runs and hikes move you along the map, with stops and postcards on the way. First to the end wins.

Golden Gate Bridge 1.7 mi · Windy City, Five Boroughs and Thames 26.2 · Death Valley 135 · Appalachian Trail 2,190 · Route 66 2,448

Games

Tic-Tac-Go

Two players, one board. Hit a square's goal to claim it, then it's their move. Three in a row wins.

2 players · Bones pot optional

Bingo

A card of steps, workouts and goals. First to finish a row, column or diagonal wins, and play goes on for the full card.

Any group

Hound'opoly

A property board game on your steps. Each day's steps pick your dice, the roll happens overnight, and you buy the parks and trails you land on. Richest at the end wins.

Any group

Rally

Badminton on your steps. Walk the shuttle's step target before its deadline to send it back a little bigger. Miss and they score. Games to 21, best of three.

One on one

Soccer

A match on everyone's steps, a day at a time. Forwards shoot, defenders block, the keeper saves. Most goals after the last day wins.

Teams of 1 to 9 · friends or bots

Cricket

Limited overs, one over a day. Each ball is the batter's steps against the bowler's: out-walk them for boundaries, fall behind and wickets fall.

Teams of 1 to 9 · friends or bots

Baseball

One inning a day. Each at-bat is your steps against the pitcher's, then the fielder's, then the catcher's at home plate. Aim left, center or right; the catcher calls a shift; big step days clear the fence.

Teams of 1 to 9 · friends or bots · 3 to 9 innings

Hoops

Basketball, a quarter a day. Your steps take the shots, one every 2,000 or so, up to five a day. Pick a drive, a mid-range or a three, out-walk whoever's guarding you to make them fall, and vote on your team's defense: pack the paint or guard the arc. Big days are dunks; three in a row and you're on fire.

Teams of 1 to 9 · friends or bots · 4 or 8 days

H-O-R-S-E

One on one. Whoever's setting picks a shot, from a 6,000-step layup to a 15,000-step half-court heave. Walk it and the other has to match it or take a letter; miss and they set the next one. Spell H-O-R-S-E (or P-I-G) and you're out.

2 players · a week or two

Habits

75 Day Challenge

A daily checklist: two workouts (one outdoors), a diet, water, reading and a progress photo. Miss a day and your streak resets, but you keep going.

75 days

Accountability

Your daily checklist, kept with friends: a workout, your steps, a quiet minute, and a line of your own. Tick everything or the day doesn't count.

Any group

Ways to play

Global Hound Challenges

Open to everyone on Hound: join from Today while it's open, no invite needed. Some have Bones prizes for the top 3, and some are sponsored.

Today → Global Hound Challenges

Pack Wars

A Global Hound Challenge for 7 to 30 days. You're put in one of four packs (Wolves, Golden Retrievers, Border Collies or Beagles), each starting on a corner of the neighborhood map. Each day, vote where your pack marches, or Hold to defend. Only members who vote add their steps that day, and overnight your pack takes the territory if it's the strongest there and beats the owner's defence. Most ground at the end wins, and the winning pack gets Bones.

Today → Global Hound Challenges → Pack Wars

Daily Doors

A door for every day. Open today's for 1 point, then do the task behind it by midnight for 2 more: your steps target (a little over last week's average), a workout, your water, all your goals, a treat for your hound, a 10-minute walk, or a cheer for someone. Hound checks it for you. Three done in a row and each one after is worth 1 more, and one missed day a week doesn't break your run. Bones behind some doors, a frame you can't buy behind the last.

Today → Global Hound Challenges → Daily Doors

Clubs

Run clubs, cycling groups and walkers. Find a public club by name, city or sport, or join a private one with its code, link or QR. A club's page shows the challenges members have posted (with a Join button), how the club did this week, and who's in it. Share a challenge with your clubs and every member gets the invite. Start your own once you reach the level it asks for; clubs show usernames, so you'll need one to join. Tap the bell on a club's page to mute it.

Clubs tab · New challenge → Share with your clubs

Community challenges

Publish a challenge that anyone with its code, link or QR code can join. Members from Tracker (level 5) with a username can publish.

Challenges → Join → Have a code?

Bones pots

Put 10, 25, 50 or 100 Bones each on a Step Race or Tic-Tac-Go. Winner takes all; a tie splits it. Only Bones earned by playing can go in.

New challenge → Step Race or Tic-Tac-Go → Pot

Boards, talk and rematches

  • Each challenge has its Board (standings, with ▲▼ for who moved since yesterday), a Talk wall for trash talk, and Details.
  • Pin one to the top of Today.
  • When it ends: Rematch with the same friends and rules, or Share result with an invite link.
Tap a challenge → Board · Talk · Details

Goals and water

Goals

Steps, distance, workouts or workout minutes, every day or every week. Hound suggests a target a little above your last four weeks.

You → Goals

The Hound reminds you

Choose how hard Hound hounds you: Gentle (one evening reminder), Persistent (up to 3 a day), Relentless (up to 5) or Off. Reminders stop once your goals are done.

You → Goals

Goal streak and Streak Shields

Every goal day pays Bones, and every 7th day in a row pays more. A Streak Shield covers one missed day. Finish every weekly goal for a weekly bonus.

Today → Goals

Water

A daily goal in cups or millilitres, a glass at a time, plus water other apps put in Health. Optional sip reminders, and your hound's bowl fills through the day.

Today → Goals → the blue +

Hound on Apple Watch

Your goal rings and streak on your wrist, and a glass of water with one tap. Open Hound on the watch, then enter the six-letter code it shows on your iPhone. Add Hound to a watch face as a complication.

You → Settings → Apple Watch

Your numbers

  • Week in review, Monday to Wednesday: last week's steps, challenges, Hound Score and Bones.
  • Today's step rank, the rest of the week.
  • Personal records: best step day, longest 10k streak, longest workout, farthest day.
  • Home screen widgets (from 0.11.0): your hound and today's goals, and steps on the iPhone Lock Screen.
Today · You → Your data

Your hound

A hound who lives the day with you, in its own yard.

Mood and care

Its mood follows your goal days over the last week. Give a treat once a day. Gentle care is the default; Classic pet adds meal hearts to keep filled.

Today → your hound · or You → Kennel

Its look and wardrobe

  • Coat, ears, tail, markings and breeds like Poodle and French Bulldog.
  • Collars, bandanas, hats and glasses; sweaters, a hoodie, a raincoat, a tutu, a superhero cape, a tuxedo; booties and sneakers.
  • Holiday drops for a little while each season (Thanksgiving, Winter from 15 November). Anything you get, you keep.
  • Tap anything to try it on in place: red ✕ puts it back, green ✓ keeps it.
Kennel → Wardrobe · Look

The Yard

It grows with your rank. Your hound strolls, naps in bed or the hammock, and drinks from a bowl that fills with your water. Swipe the grass to throw its toy. Trophies you win in challenges stand in it.

Kennel → Yard

Houses

From the Hound House to an Igloo (300 Bones), Beach Bungalow (800), Modern Glass House (900), Treehouse (1,000), Castle (1,500) and the Doghouse Mansion (2,500).

Kennel → Yard → House

The Porch

Round the back of the house, from Hunter (level 10): a Hot Tub, Lounge Chair, Porch Swing, Fire Pit, Patio Umbrella and Grill.

Kennel → swipe the sky across, or Yard → Porch

The room

Inside the house, open to everyone: a Fireplace (lit in the evening and all winter), Sofa, Bookshelf, Photo Wall with your hound's portrait, Fireside Cushion, Cozy Rug, Floor Lamp and Potted Fern, from 150 to 900 Bones.

Kennel → swipe on past the Porch, or Yard → Room

Tricks

High Five, Spin, Roll Over and Play Dead. Double-tap your hound to see one; friends can do it in your yard too.

Kennel → More → Tricks

Fitness, traits, age and a birthday

  • Fitness 1 to 10 from active days (5,000 steps). It only goes up, and a fitter hound is livelier.
  • Nine traits from your habits, kept for good: Early bird, Night owl, Lunch stroller, Trail dog, Well watered, Weekend warrior, Steady paws, Collector, Green paws.
  • A new hound starts as a puppy and grows up with your steps.
  • Pick its birthday. On the day, the yard has a party and a friend's hound comes round.
Kennel → Walks → About

Your hound as your avatar

It replaces your initials on leaderboards, friends and chats.

Kennel → Use as my avatar

Walks, care and friends

Your walks become your hound's walks, with nothing to start or stop.

Walk recaps

Ten minutes or more of steady steps is a walk: "Morning walk · 8:06 AM · 1.5 mi · 32 min".

Kennel → Walks

Walk finds and the collection book

Every finished walk brings something home, up to 5 a day. 22 kinds: a good stick, a feather, an old tennis ball… and seasonal ones like a golden acorn or a perfect snowflake. Longer walks find rarer things.

Kennel → Walks → Open the collection book

Your hound's diary

A line a day in its own voice, from your walks, finds, steps and water: "A long evening walk: 3.7 mi. My paws are tired." Only you see it, unless you share the week.

Kennel → Walks → Diary

The garden

Three plots in the Yard. Plant a seed and your steps water it: a daisy blooms at 20,000 steps, a giant sunflower at 70,000. A day off pauses it; nothing wilts. One free seed a week, and seed packs for Bones.

Kennel → Yard → Garden

Bath day

A long walk (30 minutes or 3,000 steps) brings your hound home muddy. A busy day (that walk, 8,000 steps or your goals done) earns a bath with bubbles, once a day, and they stay fresh until the next long walk.

Kennel → Care

Homemade treats

Bake Pumpkin biscuits, a Peanut butter pupcake, Frosty paws or a Birthday cake from your walk finds plus a few Bones. Feed one as today's treat, or send it to a friend's hound with a note.

Kennel → Care → Kitchen

Playdates

On a day you and a friend have both finished your goals, their hound comes over to play in your yard. Friends you're in a challenge with come first.

Kennel → Yard

Walks together and the dog park

When your walk overlaps a friend's by 10 minutes or more, it says who with. The dog park shows friends' hounds that have been out walking in the last day, with yours once you've walked. Tap one to visit.

Kennel → Walks → Dog park

Rewards

Hound Score, XP and ranks

Hound Score grows with your results and never goes down. XP from playing, opening Hound and your goals fills your level. Ranks: Pup, Tracker (level 5), Hunter (10), Alpha (15), Legend (20).

You

Bones

  • Earned from every finished challenge, levelling up, the daily bonus, goal days, Bone Dig and achievements.
  • Get a Bones pack, or, when it's on, watch a short ad for a few more.
  • Spent on your hound, its yard and garden, your look, Streak Shields, treats and bounties.
The Bones pill at the top of the Kennel

Daily bonus and Bone Dig

Open Hound each day for a bonus, bigger on every 7th day in a row. Finish your daily goals and dig once for Bones or a piece toward Dig items you can't buy.

Today

Achievements

One-time badges that each pay Bones: first finish and first win, a Bingo blackout, a perfect Daily Streak, big step days and weeks, water days, levels.

You → Achievements

Your look

Avatar frames, backgrounds and icons, unlocked by level or bought with Bones.

You → Your look

Friends

Add friends

Search by name, pick from People you may know, share your code or QR code, enter or scan theirs, invite by email, or tap Add next to anyone on a leaderboard.

Friends tab

Cheer each other on

  • Friend activity: friends' wins and achievements, with kudos.
  • Head-to-head: your record against a friend.
  • Nudges, once a day, and trash talk on each challenge's wall.
Friends tab → a friend

Bounties

Put Bones on a step target. The first friend to reach it today takes them; nobody by midnight and they come back to you.

Today → Bounties → Post one

Visits and gifts

Visit a friend's yard (your hound comes too), leave a pawprint or a treat, and send their hound a gift with a note.
