New · Intermittent fasting

Fast with your friends

Tap Start when you finish eating, on your plan: 14:10, 16:8, 18:6, 20:4 or one meal a day. The dial fills through the stages, settling, fat burning and ketosis, and tells you what's next and when. See who's fasting now and cheer them on, start a pack fast together, and join the Intermittent Fasting club with everyone on Hound. Bones for every fast and for your streak, with rest days that don't break it.

  • What you can drink (black coffee, tea, water), and a heads-up as each stage begins
  • The week's board ranks days on plan, not hours: consistency wins
  • For ages 16 and up, with a check before your first fast

New · Move points

Every move counts

Steps aren't the only way to move. Move points count all of it: one point is one step's worth, walks and runs earn their steps, and every other workout earns points a minute by how hard it is, from yoga to strength to HIIT. No watch? Start a guided routine, log a strength session set by set, or log a class, a game or the yard work by hand. A watch workout with no type is scored by your heart rate, minute by minute, so three easy hours don't outscore a hard one.

  • Guided routines (5 to 18 minutes, bodyweight, a chair or dumbbells) and strength logs with your bests
  • Movement snacks: 250 steps in 10 minutes for a few Bones, with nudges when you pick
  • Move together: do a routine with friends, each on your own phone, and all get a bonus
  • Step races can count Move points, so a cyclist or a lifter can race a walker

Goals and water

Hit your daily goals

Set goals for steps, miles and workouts, and log water with one tap. Watch them fill up as Apple Health or Health Connect syncs, keep your streak alive, and pick how hard your hound hounds you: Gentle, Playful, or Roast me.

Your hound

A hound with a life of its own

Every goal day and challenge earns Bones. Spend them on outfits, a house, and a porch with a hot tub. Then watch your hound live: walks that turn up finds for its collection book, a garden that grows with your steps, bath day, playdates at the dog park, and a birthday party every year. It grows with you, from Pup to Legend, and every new rank is a transformation under the moon: a cut scene, and a crescent, a wolfish mane, glowing eyes and finally a moonlight aura.
