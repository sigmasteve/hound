Challenges

Challenge your friends

Fifteen ways to compete on the steps, miles and workouts you'd do anyway, from a simple Step Race to a full game of Baseball. Nudge someone who's slacking, talk a little trash, and play with friends, your club, or everyone on Hound.

Clubs

Bring your whole club

Start a club for your run club, cycling group or walking crew. Post challenges to it and everyone gets the invite, and see the miles you all put in this week. Find public clubs by name, city or sport, or join a private one with its code or QR.

Goals and water

Hit your daily goals

Set goals for steps, miles and workouts, and log water with one tap. Watch them fill up as Apple Health or Health Connect syncs, keep your streak alive, and pick how hard your hound hounds you: Gentle, Playful, or Roast me.

Your hound

A hound with a life of its own

Every goal day and challenge earns Bones. Spend them on outfits, a house, and a porch with a hot tub. Then watch your hound live: walks that turn up finds for its collection book, a garden that grows with your steps, bath day, playdates at the dog park, and a birthday party every year. It grows with you, from Pup to Legend.

Celebrations

Celebrate every win

Confetti and a party horn when you hit your goals. Miss a day? Oh snap. Pick Playful, or Roast me if you can take it.

Apple Watch and widgets

On your wrist and Home Screen

Hound on Apple Watch shows your goal rings and streak, and adds a glass of water with a tap. Put it on your watch face as a complication. On your phone, widgets for your Home Screen and Lock Screen, on Android too.
