Challenges

Challenge your friends

Steps, miles and workouts, on live leaderboards. Nudge someone who's slacking, talk a little trash, and jump into weekly challenges with everyone on Hound.

Goals

Hit your daily goals

Set goals for steps, miles and workouts. Watch them fill up as Apple Health or Health Connect syncs, keep your streak alive, and wake your hound up for the day.

Your hound

Earn Bones. Spoil your hound.

Every goal day and challenge earns Bones. Spend them in the Kennel on hats, crowns, glasses and coats. Your hound grows with you, from Pup to Legend.

Celebrations

Celebrate every win

Confetti and a party horn when you hit your goals. Miss a day? Oh snap. Pick Playful, or Roast me if you can take it.

Widgets

Right on your Home Screen

Goals, steps and your hound at a glance, on your Home Screen and Lock Screen. On Android too.

Sharing

Share your wins

Story-ready cards for your week, your goals and your challenge wins, with a code that lets friends add you in one scan.
