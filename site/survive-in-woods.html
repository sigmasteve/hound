The core loop

Chop, burn, hunt, cook, survive

Days are for gathering and building. Nights are for staying in the firelight. Day and night length are both adjustable (3 minutes each by default).

  1. 🪓 Chop treesLogs go in your Log Pouch.
  2. 🔥 Feed the fireLogs keep it burning and level it up.
  3. 🏹 Hunt animalsRabbits, deer and boar drop raw meat.
  4. 🍳 Cook at the fireRaw meat becomes a 🍲 meal.
  5. 🍲 EatStay fed and your health comes back.
  6. 🌙 Survive the nightMonsters can't enter the firelight — while the fire stays above 15%.

Nights, monsters, bosses and weather

What comes out in the dark

Monsters get stronger every night: +40% health and +25% damage per night through night 10, then the climb eases off so 100-night games stay playable. Every health bar is drawn in blocks, one block per hit.

🐺Monsters

  • Shadow Wolves and Zombies from night 1
  • Ghosts from night 2
  • An Ogre stomps in from night 3
  • Go-kart engines draw wolves even by day

If the fire drops below 15% fuel, the light stops protecting camp and monsters raid it and stomp the fire.

👹Boss nights

Every 3rd night and always the final night, with a big health bar across the top of the screen.

  • 🧌 Forest Troll: slams the ground and shakes the screen
  • 🐺 Shadow Wolf King: howls to call in its wolf pack

Bosses walk straight through the firelight to wreck the fire. Beat one and everyone gets +50 🪙, logs, 3 meals and full health.

🌦️Weather

Rolled fresh for every day and every night; the first day is always clear.

  • ☀️ Clear (45%)
  • 🌧️ Rain (25%): fires burn 1.5× faster, thunder and lightning
  • 🌫️ Fog (15%): short sight, the map hides far monsters
  • ❄️ Snow (15%): walk 15% slower, get hungry 30% faster

💤Knocked out

A downed hero waits for a teammate to tap Help up, or wakes back at camp at sunrise. The game is lost only when everyone is down.

The campfire, the camps and building

Grow the fire, grow the camp

Every log burned levels the fire up, widening the safe circle and letting it hold more wood. Levels 1–5 come quickly (6, 15, 28, 45 logs), then each level takes 20 more.

Safe-circle radius by fire level

Drawn to scale from the game's formula: +90 per level to Lv 5, then +50 per level. Ringed bars mark where a new camp appears.

🏕️New camps

At main-fire levels 5, 10 and 15 a new campsite appears somewhere in the woods: Camp 2, 3 and 4. Light its fire to claim it. Each one is a full camp with its own fire, levels, safe circle and raids, and you can build there too.

🌲The world

A round forest about 7,200 points across with 810 trees, 50 animals, 7 fishing ponds and a racetrack around the edge. The same forest grows on every device from one shared seed.

Things to build (🔨 menu)

Camp buildings are placed by walking a see-through preview to a spot in any lit camp: green means it fits. Building uses logs from your pouch, then the camp chest.

Rides and racing

Four rides and a racetrack

Every ride goes 4× walking speed, rams animals and monsters, and lets you attack from the seat. Every class drives at the same speed, so races are fair.

🏎️ Go-Kart

The starter ride. Two seats, so a teammate can hop in the back.

Everyone has it

🏍️ Motorcycle

15% faster, one seat.

Unlock: win 3 races

🚤 Speedboat

Drives on land and across ponds, and is 1.3× faster on the water.

Unlock: catch 20 fish

🛸 Spaceship

Hovers over trees and ponds, so you can go in a straight line anywhere.

Unlock: beat 5 bosses

🏁 Racing

  • A dirt track with red-and-white curbs circles the forest; laps run counter-clockwise from the checkered START line.
  • Drive to the line and tap Start race: 3… 2… 1… GO! for everyone parked there.
  • 1 to 10 laps (Settings). A purple bar shows lap, time and your place.
  • 7 glowing ⚡ boost pads give 1.7× speed for 1.6 s.
  • 💫 Zap a nearby racer with the attack button and their ride spins out (no damage).
  • Results for everyone at the finish, and a personal record when racing alone.
Pets

Four companions, all at once if you like

🐕 Dog

Follows you, chases animals, fights monsters at night and fetches meat into your bag.

🐈 Cat

Finds 3–6 🪙 in the grass about once a minute, and halves the daily treasure's search circle.

🦉 Owl

Circles overhead. At night it calls out which way monsters are coming from; you see farther in the dark, and fog can't hide monsters from you.

🐎 Horse

Tap Ride: 2× walking speed and quiet, so it doesn't draw monsters the way an engine does.

Mini-games

A change of pace

Solo breaks that pay out real help for surviving. The daily treasure and lost animal are in the same place for everyone, so friends can go together.

🎣 Fishing

Walk up to any of the 7 ponds. Cast, wait for the bite, tap fast, then hold REEL to keep the fish in the green zone.

  • 🐟 Perch: 1 meat
  • 🐠 Big Trout: 2 meat
  • 🐡 Pufferfish: 10 🪙 + 1 meat
  • ✨ Golden Fish: 25 🪙, full health and a full belly

🎯 Target practice

30 seconds of tapping targets before they shrink; quick taps score 2, tapping a 🦇 bat costs 2.

  • 1 🪙 per point
  • Score 12+: Sharpshooter, 1.5× damage for 3 minutes
  • Score 22+: Sharpshooter for 6 minutes

🗺️ Daily treasure

A yellow circle on the map shows roughly where it's buried; the hint gets warmer as you get close. Dig it up for 12 logs, 25 🪙, 2 meals and either a free weapon (30%) or 15 more 🪙.

🐾 Lost animal

A baby deer or bunny is lost each day. Tap Lead home and bring it to its mom before dark for 30 🪙, 3 meals and full health.

Playing together

Up to four players, in the same room or across town

One device runs the shared world (trees, animals, monsters, fires, buildings, the clock); every player keeps their own hero, health and bags.

🏕️ Same Wi‑Fi

  • Host a Camp shows a 3-emoji camp code, like 🦊🍄🔥
  • Join by tapping the camp or typing its code
  • Drop in mid-game, or come back after leaving
  • If the camp leader leaves the app, another player takes over and the game keeps going

🌐 Online with Game Center

  • Tap 🌐 Online → invite Game Center friends (invite-only, so strangers can't join)
  • Friends tap the invite notification and land in the lobby
  • Same game, same rules; saved camps can be continued online too
  • Parents control it with Screen Time's Multiplayer Games setting

🤝 Built for teams

  • Team strip with everyone's health
  • Ride in a friend's back seat
  • See each other's pets, outfits and rides
  • Shared messages: "Dad caught a ✨ Golden Fish!"
Trophies, style and saves

Something to work toward

🏆 23 achievements

Kept on each device, so every kid has their own trophy case. They track nights survived, wins, bosses, monsters, logs, meals, fish, treasure, rescues, races, fire level, building, pets, target score and new camps.

🎨 Unlockable style

  • 7 hero outfits: Forest, Midnight, Frost, Ruby, Golden, Shadow, Starlight
  • 7 kart paints: blue, green, purple, gold, pink, orange, flame black
  • 3 rides: motorcycle, speedboat, spaceship

💾 Save & continue

  • Autosaves every minute and when the app goes to the background
  • Pause → Save & Quit (in co-op, everyone saves)
  • ▶️ Continue brings back the whole world: fires, camps, buildings, chopped trees, weather and each player's bags
Controls, screen and settings

Made for small hands

🕹️ Controls

  • Floating joystick: touch anywhere in the lower-left and it appears under your thumb
  • Big action buttons that change with what's nearby: Chop, Attack, Add Log, Cook, Eat, Drive, Ride, Fish, Dig…
  • Works on iPad and iPhone (the screen layout shrinks to fit)

🗺️ On screen

  • Health, hunger and fire bars
  • Minimap (tap to enlarge) and a compass back to camp
  • Folding backpack with logs, food, coins and camp storage
  • Hints for weather, treasure, rescues and boosts

⚙️ Settings

  • Day and night length: 0.5–10 minutes each
  • Nights to survive: 1–100
  • Race laps: 1–10
  • Sound effects on or off; all sounds and the forest and cricket ambience are synthesized in code
