What's new in Hound

Hound 1.2 is here: Pack Activities for walking and running together, your own dog as your picture, Golf and the Hound Open, and a way to log a workout without a watch. Plus Clubs, Pack Wars and Daily Doors with everyone on Hound, nine new games including Hoops, Soccer and Baseball, Hound on Apple Watch, and a hound with a whole life in the Kennel. Here's everything, and where to find it.

Start here

  1. 1

    Get the latest version

    On iPhone, update Hound from the App Store. On Android, update Hound from Google Play. Then fully close the app (swipe it away) and open it again: new features download on their own when you open it.

  2. 2

    Find your club

    Open the new Clubs tab and search for your run club, cycling group or walkers by name, city or sport. Not there yet? Start it.

  3. 3

    Got an Apple Watch?

    Open Hound on the watch, then on your iPhone go to You → Settings → Apple Watch and enter the code it shows.

New this week: fast with your friends

Already in the app, nothing to download: open Hound to get it.

Intermittent fasting

New

Tap Start when you finish eating (or set the time you stopped, up to 12 hours back), on your plan: 12:12 up to one meal a day. The dial fills through the stages, each with its own badge and colour: settling at 4 hours, fat burning at 12, ketosis at 16. Next up tells you what's coming and when, and your phone tells you as each stage begins. What to drink lists what keeps a fast: water, black coffee, plain tea. Bones once a day for a fast (5 at 12 hours up to 25 at 20, nothing more after that) and for your streak, with up to three rest days a week and one free miss. For ages 16 and up, with a check before your first fast.

Today → Goals → Try intermittent fasting · then its own tile on Today

Fast together

New

See which friends are fasting now and cheer them on. Start a pack fast and invite friends and family: each fasts their own plan, quick notes keep the pack going (water break, you got this), and when everyone who joined ends on plan, you all get a bonus. The week's board ranks you and your friends by days on plan, not hours, so consistency wins; longest fasts of 24 hours or more show as "24 h+" until tapped. Tap a friend to compare.

Fasting → Start together · Fasting now · the board

Moon forms

New

Every new rank is now a transformation. At Tracker, Hunter, Alpha and Legend your hound sits on a hill under its own moon (a crescent over a meadow, a half moon in the pines, a full moon over the ridge, a blue moon in an aurora), the moonlight finds it, and it changes and howls. It keeps the change: a crescent on its brow, a wolfish mane, glowing eyes, and a moonlight aura with stars. Already past level 5? You'll be offered your scenes once, and every one you've reached plays again from the Kennel. Prefer your plain hound? There's a switch.

At each new rank · Kennel → your hound → Moon forms

Long Shot

New

Steel plates out to 3,000 yards, on your steps: every 1,000 steps is a round, up to 15 a day, plus a round for every 20 minutes of workout. Read the three wind flags, hold off in the scope, and fire in the natural pause of your breath. Hound flies every round for real, with the day's air and wind the same for everyone. A gold plate, a data card that learns your hits, and Moon days with a sixth of the gravity.

Create → With your group → Long Shot

Bullseye

New

Archery for the whole family. Every 1,500 steps is a round of three arrows. Pick your bow, read the flags in the wind, draw and hold steady, then watch your arrow fly in slow motion. Five targets out to 90 m, the flag at 150 m, Robin Hoods, a gold balloon worth Bones, Balloon days and Moon days.

Create → With your group → Bullseye

Hound Bout

New

Boxing, one on one, a round a day. Your steps are your actions: throw combinations by tapping the openings as they light up, slip their punches, set your guard, and counter for stars. Three knockdowns in a round is a KO. Replay every round.

Create → 1 on 1 → Hound Bout

Title Bout and the Shootout

New

Two weeks with everyone on Hound. Title Bout: five days on the boxing ladder, then the top two fight for the belt, and you pick the champion for Bones. The Shootout: your steps buy penalty kicks, top corners count double, and the keeper learns your habits.

Today → Global Hound Challenges, while one's on

Improved

Short on time? Auto plays what you have left in boxing, Paw Slots, the Derby and the Shootout (playing it yourself still scores more). Hound Chess gets a clock in the stands, your hound under it, Undo for 5 seconds after a move, and your opponent's move showing up live.

Earlier this month: play it yourself

Already in the app, nothing to download: open Hound to get it.

The Prize Counter

New

Your Paw Coins keep going. When a Paw Slots challenge ends, every coin you finished with moves into your Paw Coin wallet, on top of your Bones by place. Spend them in the Kennel's new Prizes tab: the Lucky 7 Tag, the Golden Hound Collar, the Gold Coin Bowl, Jackpot Confetti for the yard, a neon sign for the Room and the Arcade Doghouse. They can't be bought with Bones, and coins never turn into Bones.

Kennel → Prizes

Paw Slots

New

A slot machine you play on your steps. Every 500 steps is a spin, up to 20 a day, plus 2 for a 20-minute workout. Play 1, 3 or 5 lines and bet up to ×5 a line for Paw Coins: three Golden Hounds pays 250, and the Collar is wild and doubles a win. Out of spins? Trade Paw Coins for more. Play solo or in packs, and when it ends the prize pool (2,500 Bones with 11 or more hounds) is paid by place. Money and Bones never go in.

Create → With your group → Paw Slots

Hound Chess

New

Real chess, with hounds for pieces, against a friend or the Hound bot. The twist: your steps today decide which pieces can move. Knights and bishops wake up at 2,500 steps, rooks at 5,000, and your queen at 7,500 or after a 20-minute workout, and yesterday's steps still count first thing in the morning. In check, everything's free. You have 24 hours a move. And every day there's a mate-in-one puzzle that opens at 5,000 steps, worth Bones.

Create → 1 on 1 → Hound Chess

Hound Roulette

New

A new game that tells you exactly what to do each day. Pick your stake, spin the wheel, and do today's task: steps, a workout, water, your goals, a hound walk or cheering friends, sized to you. Points ride and grow each day you keep it going; bank them before you spin, or risk them for more. Raid a friend who's riding high, or call them out to a Duel on tomorrow's steps. Most banked wins the Lucky Bandana for your hound, and every banked point becomes a Bone.

Create → Hound Roulette (on your own, 1 on 1 or with a group)

Cricket: bat and bowl yourself

New

Every 1,000 steps readies a ball, up to the six in the day's over. Batting, tap the ball as it reaches you: on time and middled goes for four or six, early or late finds a fielder or the edge, and you can leave one outside off (not one on the stumps). Bowling, pick pace, spin or a yorker, tap your spot on the pitch and let it go as the ring closes. Balls you don't play still play for you that night.

Create → Cricket → Bat and bowl yourself

Step Races you can watch

New

A Step Race is now a race: everyone's hound runs in its own lane, with checkpoint flags on the way to the finish. Each morning, How yesterday went plays the day back with every overtake. You get a rival to catch each day and a daily bounty for a star, and the creator can black out the standings for the last day before the finish is revealed with a podium. A Distance pool walks a trail up a mountain together.

Any Step Race or Distance pool

Baseball for real: hit it, pitch it, steal second

New

Read the grip in the pitcher's glove (fastball, curveball or changeup), then tap where the ball crosses the plate, as it gets there. Take the mound and pitch to their batters: pick the pitch, tap your spot, let it go as the ring closes. Set your jump to steal second for tonight. Up to six at-bats a day, and the Home Run Derby swings the same way.

A Baseball game made with Take my own at-bats → Take your at-bats

Hoops: take your own shots

New

As your steps earn shots, take them yourself: pick a drive, mid-range or three, tap Shoot, and release in the green. Your defender's steps narrow the green. Shots you don't take still play for you overnight.

Create → Hoops → Take my own shots

Baseball: take your own at-bats

New

One at-bat for every 3,000 steps. Watch the pitch, swing as it crosses the plate, and take the count: balls, strikes and fouls. Early pulls it, late goes the other way, and a strength workout adds power. Your at-bat counts the next time you're up in the order.

Create → Baseball → Take my own at-bats

H-O-R-S-E: call your shot

New

Shoot from anywhere on the court or up on the bench, and chain your calls: off the floor, off the scoreboard, off the glass, behind the back, nothing but net. Big step days unlock the stands, the rafters, the parking lot and a rooftop across the street. Turns pass back and forth, 24 hours each; four- and five-star makes go straight to the challenge's Talk. Add stakes, and the loser bakes the winner's hound a treat.

Create → H-O-R-S-E → Take my own shots

The Home Run Derby

New

A Global Hound Challenge: a week at the plate with everyone on Hound. One swing per 1,000 steps, up to 20 a day; your steps and workouts add distance, up to 500 feet. 475 feet or more is a Moonshot and counts twice, and a golden ball counts double. Most home runs wins, with prizes down to 10th, for the longest home run, and for 10 or more.

Pack Activities

New

A live session for a walk, run or hike together. Start one, pick when and how long, and invite friends or open it to your club. While it's on, the board shows everyone's steps, scent-trail puzzles open as the pack's steps add up, and anyone can start a quick Dash or Pack push. Turn on the live map and the pack sees each other's spots, and Bones to walk to. Your spot is shared with that pack only, and deleted when it ends.

Golf

New

Nine or eighteen holes on four courses, and you take every shot. Your steps bank yards (10 steps a yard) and your workouts bank power. Pull back on the course to aim, the caddie picks the club, and tap Hit as the marker crosses the middle. Play stroke play, skins or match play, up to 12 players.

Create → Golf

The Hound Open

New

Golf with everyone on Hound: 18 holes on one course over a week, stroke play, with a leaderboard you can flip between the top of the field, your friends and your club. Join in the first three days; your steps count from the day you join. The top three win Bones, and everyone who finishes gets some too.

Today → Global Hound Challenges, while one's on

Workouts in the sports games

New

In Soccer, Cricket, Baseball, Rally, Hoops and H-O-R-S-E, 15 minutes of strength training earns a bonus (a second at 45 minutes), and playing the real sport for 20 minutes or more makes it a game day, with a "Played for real" tag.

Any sports game

Log a workout

New

No watch? Log a workout yourself: up to two a day, up to 90 minutes each, today or yesterday. It counts in full for your goals and Daily Doors, and at half value in the sports games and Golf. It never adds steps or miles.

Today → Goals → Log a workout

Pack Wars

New

A Global Hound Challenge for four packs: Wolves, Golden Retrievers, Border Collies and Beagles. Each day, vote where your pack marches on the neighborhood map, or hold to defend. Only members who vote add their steps that day, so every vote counts. Most ground at the end wins, and the whole winning pack gets Bones.

Today → Global Hound Challenges

Daily Doors

New

A door a day, like an advent calendar. Open it for a point, then do the task behind it by midnight for two more: your steps, a workout, your water, a treat for your hound, a walk or a cheer. Hound checks it for you. Three days in a row and each door is worth more. Bones are behind some doors, and the last one hides a frame you can't buy.

Today → Global Hound Challenges

Game of Tag, as a daily chase

Improved

One hound is It each day. Everyone else gets a head start, and whoever It out-walks is tagged. The slowest of them is It tomorrow. Watch each day back on a track, through the woods, up a mountain or along the beach.

Create → Game of Tag

New games

Nine new ways to compete, all on real steps. The sports take teams of 1 to 9, with friends or bots.

Hoops and H-O-R-S-E

New

Hoops: basketball a quarter a day. Your steps take the shots; pick a drive, a mid-range or a three, out-walk whoever's guarding you, and vote on your team's defense. Big days are dunks, three in a row and you're on fire. H-O-R-S-E: one on one. The setter picks a shot with a step target; make it, and the other has to match it or take a letter.

Create → Hoops · Create → H-O-R-S-E

Soccer, Cricket and Baseball

New
  • Soccer: a match on everyone's steps, a day at a time. Forwards shoot, defenders block, the keeper saves.
  • Cricket: limited overs, one over a day. Out-walk the bowler for boundaries.
  • Baseball: one inning a day. Aim left, center or right; big step days clear the fence.
Create → pick a game

Rally and Hound'opoly

New
  • Rally: badminton on your steps. Walk the shuttle back before its deadline. Games to 21, best of three.
  • Hound'opoly: your steps pick your dice, the roll happens overnight, and you buy the parks and trails you land on.
Create → pick a game
