Hound beta · Sept 28 – 30

What's new in Hound

You can now cheer on and nudge your friends, join challenges open to everyone, earn badges and a daily bonus, and dress up your avatar for Halloween. Here's everything, and where to find it.

Start here

  1. 1

    Get the latest version

    Update Hound in TestFlight if it offers one. Then fully close the app (swipe it away) and open it again. New features download on their own when you open it.

  2. 2

    Turn on notifications

    Tap Turn on when Today asks, or go to Settings → Notifications. Without them you'll miss friend requests, nudges and your Tic-Tac-Go turns.

  3. 3

    Try a nudge

    Open any challenge, tap a friend on the leaderboard, and send a 🔥 or a nudge. Then tell us how it felt.

Cheer each other on

New ways to keep your friends moving, and to find more of them.

Reactions

New

Tap anyone on a challenge leaderboard and react to their progress with 🔥 💪 👏 😮. Reactions show under each person's name for the day, and yours are outlined. Tap one again to take it back. The first reaction you give someone each day sends them a notification.

Any challenge → Leaderboard → tap a person

Nudges

New

Give a friend a push to get moving: "Jordan nudged you 👋". You can nudge each person once a day. Nudges you get also show up on your Today screen, so you won't miss one.

Leaderboard → tap a person → Nudge · or a friend's page → Nudge 👋

Find people

New

Search for anyone on Hound by name, and see "People you may know": people in your challenges, friends of friends, and people who just joined. Would rather not be found? Turn off Show me in Find people in Settings.

Friends tab → Find people

Inviting friends who don't have Hound yet

Improved

Your friend link now walks people through getting the beta first (TestFlight on iPhone, or the Android download), then gives them your invite code. New people can enter that code on their Today screen to connect with you right away.

Friends tab → Share · Today → Got an invite code?

More friend features

Improved
  • A friend activity feed on the Friends tab.
  • Add someone as a friend straight from a challenge leaderboard.
  • A cleaner Friends page, with the add options up front.
  • You now see everyone's avatar frames, backgrounds and icons.
  • Tapping a friend request notification opens it, ready to accept.

Challenges

Global Hound Challenges

New

Challenges open to everyone on Hound. Join from Today while the join window is open, and see how you stack up against the whole pack.

Today → Global Hound Challenges

Tic-Tac-Go, fairer and more flexible

Improved
  • Workouts and steps from before your turn now count, from the last 24 hours by default.
  • Whoever creates a game picks the window: 12, 24 or 48 hours.
  • Claiming a square with a workout no longer resets your step count, and a workout can't be used twice.
  • Hikes now count as walks.
Create → Tic-Tac-Go → Activity counts from

Rematch, share and leaderboard movement

New

When a challenge ends, start a rematch with the same group in one tap, or share how you did. Leaderboards now show ▲▼ arrows for who moved up or down since yesterday.

A finished challenge → Rematch / Share result

Unlockable challenge types

New

Some challenge types unlock as you earn Bones, so there's always something new to work toward.

Create → pick a challenge type

Rewards and your look

Daily bonus and streak

New

Open Hound each day for free Bones. Keep a streak going for a bigger bonus on day 7. Tap Remind me tomorrow so you don't break it.

Today → first open of the day

Achievements

New

One-time badges for things like your first win, a 7-day streak or walking 10,000 steps in a day. Each one pays Bones, and your friends can see yours on your profile.

Settings → Achievements

Get started checklist

New

New to Hound? Connect your health app, add a friend and join a challenge to earn a Bones reward.

Today → Get started

Halloween drop

New

Spooky frames, backgrounds and icons in the Shop, only until October 31.

Locker → Shop → Halloween

Avatar icons and quicker Locker access

New

Pick an icon to show on your avatar in place of your initials. The Locker now has its own hanger button at the top of every screen, and there's an Add Bones shortcut next to your Shop balance.

Top bar → hanger icon

Today and your data

Weekly recap

New

A quick look back at your week, right on Today.

Today → Your week in review

Clearer weekly numbers

Improved

Your data now says "Last 7 days", so it's clear what the totals cover. The daily average counts only finished days, so today's partial steps don't pull it down.

Your data → Last 7 days

Help

New

A how-to guide for every challenge type and feature, with search.

Notifications that work for you

Improved

Hound now tells you when your phone isn't set up for notifications, and how to fix it. Tapping a notification takes you straight to the right challenge or friend. Choose which ones you get in Settings → Notifications.

Settings → Notifications
