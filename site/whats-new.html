What's new in Hound

Clubs for your run club, Pack Wars and Daily Doors with everyone on Hound, seven new games including Soccer, Cricket and Baseball, Hound on Apple Watch, and a hound with a whole life in the Kennel. Here's everything, and where to find it.

Start here

  1. 1

    Get the latest version

    On iPhone, update Hound from the App Store. On Android, download the latest from houndchallenge.net/get. Then fully close the app (swipe it away) and open it again: new features download on their own when you open it.

  2. 2

    Find your club

    Open the new Clubs tab and search for your run club, cycling group or walkers by name, city or sport. Not there yet? Start it.

  3. 3

    Got an Apple Watch?

    Open Hound on the watch, then on your iPhone go to You → Settings → Apple Watch and enter the code it shows.

Play together

Your club, and everyone on Hound.

Clubs

New

A fifth tab for run clubs, cycling groups and walkers. Find a public club by name, city or sport, or join a private one with its code, link or QR. A club's page shows the challenges members have posted, with a Join button, the miles everyone put in this week, and who's in it. Making a challenge? Share it with your clubs and every member gets the invite. Start your own once you reach the level it asks for.

Clubs tab

Pack Wars

New

A Global Hound Challenge for four packs: Wolves, Golden Retrievers, Border Collies and Beagles. Each day, vote where your pack marches on the neighborhood map, or hold to defend. Only members who vote add their steps that day, so every vote counts. Most ground at the end wins, and the whole winning pack gets Bones.

Today → Global Hound Challenges

Daily Doors

New

A door a day, like an advent calendar. Open it for a point, then do the task behind it by midnight for two more: your steps, a workout, your water, a treat for your hound, a walk or a cheer. Hound checks it for you. Three days in a row and each door is worth more. Bones are behind some doors, and the last one hides a frame you can't buy.

Today → Global Hound Challenges

Game of Tag, as a daily chase

Improved

One hound is It each day. Everyone else gets a head start, and whoever It out-walks is tagged. The slowest of them is It tomorrow. Watch each day back on a track, through the woods, up a mountain or along the beach.

Create → Game of Tag

Holiday themes

New

Snow, falling leaves or confetti drift over Today for Thanksgiving, Christmas, New Year and Lunar New Year, with a countdown and a little trim on your tiles. Turn the motion off in Settings → Today.

Today, while a holiday is on

New games

Seven new ways to compete, all on real steps. The sports take teams of 1 to 9, with friends or bots.

Soccer, Cricket and Baseball

New
  • Soccer: a match on everyone's steps, a day at a time. Forwards shoot, defenders block, the keeper saves.
  • Cricket: limited overs, one over a day. Out-walk the bowler for boundaries.
  • Baseball: one inning a day. Aim left, center or right; big step days clear the fence.
Create → pick a game

Rally and Hound'opoly

New
  • Rally: badminton on your steps. Walk the shuttle back before its deadline. Games to 21, best of three.
  • Hound'opoly: your steps pick your dice, the roll happens overnight, and you buy the parks and trails you land on.
Create → pick a game

Journeys

New

Walk a famous course with your own miles: the Golden Gate Bridge, a marathon through Chicago, New York or London, Death Valley, the Appalachian Trail or Route 66, with a postcard at every stop. Race friends along it, or walk one on your own from the You tab.

Create → Journey · You → Journey

Accountability

New

Your own daily checklist, kept with friends: a workout, your steps, a quiet minute, and a line of your own.

Create → Accountability

Your hound's life

The Kennel now has three tabs, and your hound has a lot more going on.

A wardrobe, a house and a porch

New

Sweaters, a hoodie, a raincoat, a cape and shoes to go with the hats and glasses, with holiday drops each season. Five houses to move into, a Porch round the back with a hot tub, and gifts with a note for a friend's hound.

Kennel → Wardrobe · Kennel → Yard · a friend's page → Send a gift

Walks, finds and a diary

New

Your walks show up as your hound's walks, and they turn up finds for its collection book. Your hound keeps a diary of your days together.

Kennel → Walks

The garden

New

Plant seeds and watch them grow with your steps. A free seed every week.

Kennel → Yard → Garden

Fitness, traits, age and a birthday

New

Your hound gets fitter on your active days, earns traits from your habits, like Early bird or Trail dog, grows up from a puppy as your steps add up, and has a party in the yard on its birthday.

Kennel → Walks → About

Bath day, treats and its room

New

Earn a bath with a long walk and give your hound a scrub and a new 'do, bake homemade treats from your walk finds, and furnish the room inside its house.

Kennel → Care · Kennel → Yard → Room

Playdates and the dog park

New

A playdate with a friend whose goals are done too, walks that overlap a friend's, and a dog park with the friends who walked today.

Kennel → Walks

Goals and your wrist

Hound on Apple Watch

New

Your goal rings and streak on your wrist, and a glass of water with one tap. Add Hound to your watch face as a complication.

You → Settings → Apple Watch

Water

New

Add a water goal and log a glass with the + on Today's Goals tile. Water from other apps counts too, and sip reminders are there if you want them.

You → Goals → Water

Maintenance heads-up

Improved

When Hound's server is down for maintenance, the app says so and when it'll be back, instead of loading forever.
